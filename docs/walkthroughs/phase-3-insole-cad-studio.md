## 3. Mathematical Foundations & Curve Geometry

### 3.1 European Shoe Sizing Length Formula
The internal footwear insole length in millimeters ($L$) is derived from the standard Paris point definition ($\frac{2}{3}\text{ cm} \approx 6.6667\text{ mm}$ per increment):
$$L(\text{size}) = \text{size} \times 6.6667 - 6.6667 \quad (\text{in mm})$$

- **EU 36:** $233.3\text{ mm}$
- **EU 40:** $260.0\text{ mm}$
- **EU 41:** $266.7\text{ mm}$
- **EU 42:** $273.3\text{ mm}$
- **EU 45:** $293.3\text{ mm}$

### 3.2 Width Proportions & Arch Support Factors
- **Forefoot / Ball Width ($W_{\text{ball}}$):** $0.36 \times L$ (e.g. EU 41 = $96.0\text{ mm}$).
- **Heel Width ($W_{\text{heel}}$):** $0.26 \times L$ (e.g. EU 41 = $69.3\text{ mm}$).
- **Arch Waist Width ($W_{\text{waist}}$):** $0.22 \times L$ (e.g. EU 41 = $58.7\text{ mm}$).
- **Arch Profile Curvature Multiplier:**
  $$\text{Multiplier} = \begin{cases} 0.85 & \text{Flat / Low Arch} \\ 1.00 & \text{Neutral / Medium Arch} \\ 1.25 & \text{High Arch Support} \end{cases}$$

### 3.3 AutoCAD R12 DXF Specification & CNC Layering
The DXF output generated by `generateDxfContent` produces strict ASCII AutoCAD R12 syntax with minimal overhead to ensure zero-loss importing in CorelDRAW 2020+, AutoCAD, and CNC Knife / Laser Cutting tables:
- `CUT_OUTLINE` (Color 7): Primary outer cutting perimeter.
- `ARCH_SUPPORT` (Color 1 - Red): Placement marking for thermoformed TPU arch bridges.
- `HEEL_CUP` (Color 3 - Green): Deep heel cup alignment guide.
- `METATARSAL` (Color 4 - Cyan): Forefoot metatarsal dome pad.
- `DIMENSIONS` (Color 5 - Blue): Inspection crosshairs and millimeter readouts.
